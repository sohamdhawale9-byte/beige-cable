The price predictor on this site runs in your browser, so it uses a lighter model fitted to the same listings: a log-linear regression on price per sqft. On the same held-out 20 percent it scores R2 0.991, MAE 18.9 L and MAPE 6.7%. The FastAPI service in the project serves the LightGBM model.
